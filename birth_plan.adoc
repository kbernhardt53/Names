== Labor

* Labor at home when not in active labor
* Pain management 
** Walk and move around as much as desired
** Use shower during labor
** Use birthing ball and peanut ball
** Option for epidural 
** Avoid analgesics if at all possible
* Partner only present (Kyle)
* Able to switch positions to whatever is comfortable (if able)
* Dim lights
* Music
* Option to take pictures during labor
* Stay hydrated by drinking clear fluids
* Monitor baby as much as medically necessary. When in doubt, monitor. 

== Birth

=== Vaginal

* Skin to skin right away
* Partner help cut cord (if appropriate)
* Delayed cord clamping (minimum 1 minute)

=== C-Section

* Have skin to skin as soon as possible after birth
* Have partner present during operation
* No clear screen

== Newborn Care 

* Breastfeed as soon as possible 
* Don't offer anything else but breast milk (unless necessary)
* Room in with baby
* Vitamin K, all routine shots and procedures
* Circumcision: Yes
