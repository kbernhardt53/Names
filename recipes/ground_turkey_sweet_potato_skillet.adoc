= 15-Minute Ground Turkey & Sweet Potato Skillet
:author: Quick & Healthy Dinner Series
:toc:
:icons: font

A nutrient-dense, one-pan dinner packed with lean protein, fiber, and complex carbohydrates.

== Recipe Overview

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 12 minutes

Servings: 2–3

== Ingredients

1 lb Lean ground turkey (93/7)

1 bag (10–12 oz) Frozen diced sweet potatoes (or microwave-steamed sweet potato cubes)

2 cups Fresh baby spinach

1 tbsp Olive oil

1 tsp Garlic powder

Salt and black pepper (to taste)

== Instructions

Brown the Turkey:
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey, season with garlic powder, salt, and black pepper, and break it up with a spatula until fully browned (about 5–6 minutes).

Add Sweet Potatoes:
Stir the frozen sweet potatoes directly into the pan. Cover with a lid, reduce heat to medium, and cook for about 5 minutes until the sweet potatoes are heated through and tender.

Wilt Spinach and Serve:
Remove the lid, fold in the fresh baby spinach, and stir continuously for 1–2 minutes until the spinach is wilted into the warm skillet mixture. Serve immediately.

== Busy Parent Tip

Using pre-diced frozen sweet potatoes eliminates all potato peeling and chopping time while preserving all nutritional benefits!
