= 15-Minute Mediterranean Chicken & Rice Bowl
:author: Quick & Healthy Dinner Series
:toc:
:icons: font

A vibrant, protein-packed bowl that uses pre-cooked brown rice to keep prep minimal and dinner on the table fast.

== Recipe Overview

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 8-10 minutes

Servings: 2

== Ingredients

1 lb Chicken tenders or thin-cut chicken breasts (cut into bite-sized pieces)

1 pouch Pre-cooked brown rice (e.g., Uncle Ben's Ready Rice)

1 Cucumber (diced)

1 can (15 oz) Chickpeas (rinsed and drained)

3-4 tbsp Store-bought Greek vinaigrette or tzatziki sauce

Salt, pepper, and oregano or garlic powder (to taste)

Olive oil (for cooking)

== Instructions

Cook Chicken:
Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a splash of olive oil. Season the chicken pieces with salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic powder or oregano. Sauté for 6–8 minutes until golden and cooked through.

Warm Rice:
Microwave the pouch of brown rice according to package directions (usually 90 seconds).

Assemble Bowls:
Divide the warm rice into two serving bowls. Top with cooked chicken, fresh diced cucumber, and drained chickpeas.

Dress and Serve:
Drizzle generously with Greek vinaigrette or spoonfuls of tzatziki sauce. Serve immediately!

== Busy Parent Tip

For even faster assembly, swap raw chicken breast for store-bought pre-cooked chicken strips or rotisserie chicken breast.
