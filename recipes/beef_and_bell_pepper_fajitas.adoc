= 15-Minute Ground Beef & Bell Pepper Fajita Bowls
:author: Quick & Healthy Dinner Series
:toc:
:icons: font

All the flavor of classic fajitas made in a quick skillet without having to roll individual tortillas.

== Recipe Overview

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Servings: 2–3

== Ingredients

1 lb Lean ground beef (or ground turkey)

1 bag (12–16 oz) Frozen sliced bell peppers and onions

1 packet Low-sodium fajita or taco seasoning

1 pouch Pre-cooked brown rice or quinoa (e.g., microwaveable pouch)

Salsa or plain Greek yogurt (for topping)

== Instructions

Brown Beef & Veggies:
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and the frozen pepper and onion mix. Cook for 6–7 minutes, breaking up the meat with a spatula, until the beef is browned and the veggies are tender.

Season:
Drain excess grease if needed. Stir in the fajita seasoning and 1/4 cup water. Simmer for 2 minutes until the sauce coats the beef and vegetables.

Assemble Bowls:
Heat the rice or quinoa pouch in the microwave according to package directions (usually 90 seconds). Divide into bowls, top with the beef and pepper mixture, and finish with a dollop of salsa or plain Greek yogurt.

== Busy Parent Tip

Using frozen pre-sliced bell peppers and onions saves chopping time and eliminates food waste!
