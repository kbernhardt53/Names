= Diapering
{docdate}

== Setup

* [ ] Get wipes ready.
* [ ] Open new diaper and place new diaper under baby with tabs in the back.
* [ ] Open old diaper and wipe around his belly button or penis to get him to pee. Hold old diaper over to catch it. 

== Cleaning

* [ ] Using old diaper, wipe the stool away from front to back.
* [ ] Clear the diaper area from front to back with an unscented wipe. 
* [ ] Pat baby's diaper area with a clean towel or let air dry (fan with another diaper). 
* [ ] Coat your baby's bottom with a thick layer of fragrant-free barrier cream that contains zinc or petroleum to prevent diaper rash. 

== Finishing Up
* [ ] Point penis down.
* [ ] Close diaper and secure tabs around baby legs.
* [ ] Ensure frills are out around legs (helps) with blow outs. 
* [ ] Fold diaper down in front to avoid cord.
